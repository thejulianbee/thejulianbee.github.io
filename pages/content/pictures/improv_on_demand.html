IMPROV ON DEMAND

This is the improv group that I'm in with Simran, Anna, Daania, Thomas, and Will. We've had two runs of shows at the Phoenix Theater so far: "Slasher On Demand" (dir. me) in October/November 2025 and "GTA 7" (dir. Daania) in March/April 2026. We also perform a show and host a jam the first Monday of every month at The Marsh Berkeley.
